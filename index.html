ABOUT THE CLUB

MORE THAN
A TEAM.

The Montclair State University Men's Rugby Club is a competitive club team representing MSU in the Tri-State Rugby Conference.

Whether you've played rugby for years or have never touched a rugby ball before, there's a place for you here.

The club welcomes players of all experience levels, including students who are completely new to rugby.
